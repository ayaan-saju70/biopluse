# Anatomy model credits

Anatomical geometry: BodyParts3D, © The Database Center for Life Science, licensed under
Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
Dataset: https://dbarchive.biosciencedbc.jp/en/bodyparts3d/download.html
Publication: Mitsuhashi et al. (2009), https://doi.org/10.1093/nar/gkn613

Obtained through the simplified, repacked copy in github.com/ashemag/human-atlas (MIT code;
data attribution as above). Adaptations in this project: selected structures merged into
named groups, converted to a single GLB, per-organ pivots added, and lung surfaces
generated procedurally (smoothed isosurface around each lung's bronchial tree and vessels),
because BodyParts3D contains no solid lung parenchyma mesh. The lung surfaces are an
approximation and not anatomical scan data.
